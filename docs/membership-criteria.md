# Work Group Membership Criteria

Membership in the AI4SDLC Work Group requires meeting the following criteria:

## Nomination and Endorsement

- Formal nomination by a sponsoring organization
- Organizational endorsement confirming support for the member's participation

## Organizational Affiliation

- Member of a DoW component, defense agency, FFRDC, or authorized industry/academic partner

## Role as AI4SDLC Champion

- Commitment to actively promoting the AI4SDLC initiative within their organization and professional networks
- Willingness to represent and advocate for AI4SDLC goals and guidance

## Early Adopter Engagement

- Ability to identify and connect potential early adopters to the initiative
- Participation in fast, light-touch engagements with early adopters to capture lessons learned and develop case studies

## Contribution Expectations

- Active Participation in working sessions and reviews
- Represent organizational perspective while advancing common goals
